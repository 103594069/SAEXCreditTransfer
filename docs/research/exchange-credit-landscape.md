# Exchange credit pre-approval: how it works today and who already does it

Researched 2026-11-10 to inform the revamp. **Method caveat:** the research session's network blocked direct fetches of rmit.edu.au, qs.com, terradotta.com, dreamapply.com, transferology.com and exchangecourse.unsw.edu.au, so findings come from search-engine extracts of the official pages. Treat each as likely, and re-check against the source page before relying on it.

## 1. RMIT's process today

- **Study Plan:** the student researches partner courses and fills in a Study Plan that pairs each host course with an RMIT equivalent or an elective. It carries course outlines (code, credit points, hours, level, topics, assessment, textbooks). It is submitted through Student Connect and forwarded to academics in the student's school or college. It must be approved before the student can enrol and go.
  - https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/life-and-work-opportunities/global-study-and-work/how-to-apply/study-plans
  - https://www.rmit.edu.au/content/dam/rmit/au/en/students/documents/careers-opportunities/study-plan-2025.pdf
- **Approver:** "a signed agreement between a student and the relevant Program Coordinator/Manager" (RMIT Vietnam wording). Melbourne pages say the "academic college/school" reviews it. Approval "may take a few weeks"; Vietnam pages say 10 to 20 working days.
  - https://www.rmit.edu.vn/students/work-and-study-opportunities/global-experience-programs/global-experience-study-plan
- **Systems:** the approved plan is uploaded to the **Mobi** portal (https://outbound.rmit.edu.au). Global Experience then nominates the student to the host. Students must enrol in exactly the approved credit points. RMIT advises listing **6 to 8 courses as backups**, since approval does not guarantee the host will offer them.
  - https://rmit.edu.au/students/my-course/enrolment/enrol-exchange-student/exchange-outbound
- **Not verified:** which vendor runs Mobi. **Not found:** any public RMIT database of previously approved courses.
- **Eligibility:**
  - GPA 2.0/4: confirmed on the Melbourne page. https://www.rmit.edu.au/study-with-us/international-students/programs-for-international-students/study-abroad-and-exchange/student-exchange
  - 72cp (Bachelor) / 36cp (Master) completed by departure: stated only on RMIT Vietnam pages, not verified for Melbourne. https://www.rmit.edu.vn/students/work-and-study-opportunities/global-experience-programs/exchange-to-partners-worldwide
  - OS-HELP "at least 12 credit points to complete at RMIT when you return": third-party source only, not verified on rmit.edu.au. Official criteria: https://www.studyassist.gov.au/help-loans/os-help-loans-and-overseas-study

## 2. Universities with searchable precedent databases

- **UQ:** public Credit Precedents database, about 28k entries, typed Credit or Exchange, with outcome and assessment date. States "a precedent does not guarantee you credit". https://creditprecedents.app.uq.edu.au
- **Melbourne:** in-house Overseas Study Planner, plus a spreadsheet of previously approved subjects. https://students.unimelb.edu.au/your-course/study-overseas/step-by-step-guides/semester-and-year-long-programs/plan-your-overseas-study
- **Deakin:** database of previously assessed units. https://deakin.edu.au/students/study-support/study-abroad/trimester-abroad/search-our-database-for-previously-assessed-units
- **UNSW:** exchange course search; whether it shows past approvals is not verified. https://exchangecourse.unsw.edu.au
- **Monash:** pre-approved unit lists per school, often a PDF per partner. https://www.monash.edu.my/study-abroad/outbound/exchange-program/pre-approved-units/pre-approved-units-engineering-students
- **US and Hong Kong:** course-equivalency databases are common, e.g. https://studyabroad.ku.edu/course-equivalencies and https://webapp.science.hku.hk/student/servlet/course_equiv

## 3. Commercial software

- **Terra Dotta:** Study Abroad Course Catalog plus a course approval workflow; approvers get emails and can approve, reject or comment. The closest existing match.
  - https://support.terradotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/37321446504087-The-Study-Abroad-Course-Catalog
  - https://services.luc.edu/TDClient/33/Portal/Requests/Service/182/Study-Abroad-Course-Approval
- **MoveON (QS) and Mobility-Online (SOP):** learning agreements as "learning components" with staff sign-off, linked to Erasmus Without Paper (EWP). No evidence of a precedent library.
  - https://uni-jena.de/unijenamedia/285153/anleitung-ola-fuer-fachkoordinierende.pdf
  - https://www.haw-landshut.de/en/international/outgoing/studium-im-ausland/anerkennung
- **Via TRM:** general application platform; no native equivalency module found. https://www.smcm.edu/international-education/?p=2789
- **DreamApply, Ex-Change:** nothing verifiable on credit pre-approval.
- **Transferology / TES (CollegeSource):** strong US domestic transfer-equivalency network (TES staff-facing, Transferology student-facing). Foreign or exchange coverage not verified. https://registrar.ucdavis.edu/node/3321
- **Erasmus Online Learning Agreement / EWP:** three-party digital learning agreement with a recognition commitment per component. Europe only; no precedent reuse found. https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2023-10/User-Guide-Erasmus-Digital-LA_en.pdf

## 4. Gaps and complaints

- ESN surveys: 28.6% of respondents did not get full recognition; in an older survey 73% changed their learning agreement after signing. https://esn.org/node/801 , https://esn.org/prime/2009
- Terra Dotta's own conference session pitches ending "running around campus with a paper form". https://terradotta.swoogo.com/tdug2021/session/458815/go-green-with-course-approvals-study-abroad
- Existing precedent databases are mostly static: non-binding, not tied to current offerings, and split into PDFs or spreadsheets per faculty.

## Implications

- At RMIT the approval itself is a form plus email, with no visible precedent search. Precedent reuse is the clearest gap.
- Integrating with existing mobility platforms (Mobi, MoveON, Mobility-Online, EWP/OLA) looks more viable than replacing them.
- The approver's job is one equivalence decision per course; precedent records need dates and review status, since course content drifts.
- Course changes after arrival are very common.
- Terra Dotta and Melbourne's Overseas Study Planner are the nearest benchmarks.

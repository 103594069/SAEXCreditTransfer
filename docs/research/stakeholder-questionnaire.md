# Questionnaire: how outbound exchange credit approval really works

**Why I'm asking:** I'm designing software to make credit pre-approval for outbound exchange faster and more consistent, modelled on RMIT's process. Before designing the workflow, I want to check my assumptions against how it actually works. There are no wrong answers; "it depends" or "I don't know" is useful too.

**Time:** about 10 to 15 minutes. Answer only your own section, plus the shared one at the end if you can.

**What I need back:** short written answers, or a 20-minute call if that's easier.

---

## A. Global Experience / exchange office staff

1. Walk me through what happens to a Study Plan from the moment a student submits it to the moment it's approved. Who touches it, in what order?
2. Does your team check anything before it reaches the academic (completeness, documents, eligibility, credit totals)? What do you most often send back to students?
3. Is the Study Plan approved as a whole, or course by course? Can some courses be approved while others are still being decided or denied?
4. When a course is denied, what happens next? Does the whole plan go back to the student, or just that course?
5. What decides who the academic approver is for a given student (program, school, college)? Roughly how many different approvers are involved across RMIT?
6. Do you keep any record of previously approved courses that you or students can look up? If so, where and in what form?
7. How long does approval usually take, and where does most of the waiting happen?
8. How often do students change courses after arriving at the partner? How is that handled today?
9. How are backup courses handled: approved up front, or only when needed?
10. What would you most want a new tool to do, and what would make you refuse to use one?

## B. Academic approvers (Program Coordinator / Manager)

1. When you assess a host course, what do you look at, in order of importance? (Topics, learning outcomes, hours, level, assessment, credit, the partner's reputation, past approvals.)
2. Do you look at whether the same course was approved before? If you could see past approvals, would you rely on them, and how old would be too old?
3. Partner courses change over time. How do you tell if a course you approved before is still the same course?
4. Do you assess each course separately, or do you look at the whole semester together? Would you ever approve a course only because of what else is in the plan?
5. Besides approve or deny, what other outcomes do you give? (Approve as elective, approve with conditions, need more information.)
6. How long does one course take you to assess, and what slows it down most?
7. Would an automated comparison of the partner course outline against the RMIT unit help, or would you distrust it? What would it need to show for you to trust it?
8. Is anyone else involved before or after your decision?

## C. Students who've done an exchange

1. How did you find courses at the partner, and how did you decide which RMIT units to match them to?
2. What was the hardest or slowest part of getting your Study Plan approved?
3. Were any courses denied or sent back? What happened then?
4. Did you change any courses after arriving? Why, and how was the change approved?
5. Did you list backup courses? Did you end up using them?
6. Would seeing which courses past students got approved have changed your choices?
7. Did you get all the credit you expected when you came back?

## D. Everyone (optional)

1. If you could fix one thing about this process, what would it be?
2. Is there anyone else I should talk to?

# The AI never makes the credit Decision

AI compares outlines and, at the Recommend level of AI Assistance Level, may suggest an Outcome with reasons, but every Decision is made and recorded by an Assessor. An auto-approve mode was considered so users could test it, and dropped: credit is a formal academic record, and TEQSA's credit and RPL guidance expects decisions to rest on evidence and academic judgement and to be recorded explicitly. Auto-approval would also make the product harder to sell, since a Home Institution's credit policy would have to change before it could be used. Do not reintroduce automated Decisions without revisiting this ADR.

## Consequences

- AI Assistance Level has two levels, Explain and Recommend; there is no Automatic level.
- Every Outcome records the Assessor who decided it, whatever the AI suggested.

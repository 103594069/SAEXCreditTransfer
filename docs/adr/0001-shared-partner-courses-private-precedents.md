# Partner Courses are shared across Home Institutions; Course Mappings and Precedents are private

The product is meant to spread across many Home Institutions, and Partner Course data (outlines, topics, Partner Credit) is the hardest data to get. Students supply it one Study Plan at a time. So one Partner Course record is shared by every Home Institution: a new customer starts with the network's catalogue instead of an empty one. Course Mappings and the Precedents they produce stay private to the Home Institution that made them, because a credit decision is only meaningful against that institution's own Home Units, and approval decisions are institutional records that universities should not have to share.

## Considered Options

- **Nothing shared:** simplest and safest for data ownership, but every new Home Institution starts from zero, which removes the network effect the product depends on.
- **Share Partner Courses and Precedents:** strongest network effect, but another university's decision doesn't map onto your Home Units, and sharing approvals raises data-sharing objections at the point of sale.

## Consequences

- The link from a Partner Course to a Home Unit cannot live on the Partner Course. It lives on a Course Mapping owned by one Home Institution.
- Partner Course records need an owner-neutral way to be corrected and kept current, since no single Home Institution owns them.
- Revisiting this later (for example, opt-in sharing of anonymised Precedent counts) is possible, but moving private data to shared is much harder than the reverse.
